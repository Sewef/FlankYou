Flank You automatically detects flanked tokens using either PTU 1.05 or D&D 5e 2014 rules.

Assign tokens to one of four teams, mark individual creatures as immune to flanking, and optionally include mounts. Flanked creatures receive a clear on-map indicator, while optional colored hitboxes and a compact token overview make every position easy to verify.
